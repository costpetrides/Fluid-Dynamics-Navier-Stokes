Potential Flow over a Semicircular Hill

A steady uniform stream flows over a semicircular hill of radius a. This visualization uses the exact potential-flow velocity field to animate tracer particles and a material contour.

Open original MP4 ↗View Python source ↗

Velocity potential

Φ(r, θ) = U(r + a²/r) cos θ

The wind approaches with uniform far-field speed U. The upper semicircle represents an impermeable hill.

Physical interpretation

The model is steady, incompressible and irrotational. At the hill summit the ideal-fluid speed is 2U, giving a pressure coefficient of −3. It excludes viscosity, separation and turbulence.
