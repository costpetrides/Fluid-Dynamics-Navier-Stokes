Potential flow over a semicircular hill

Φ(r, θ) = U(r + a²/r) cos θ

Steady, incompressible, irrotational potential flow in the upper half-plane. The colored field is the normalized speed |u|/U; small white dots are moving fluid tracers. The solid semicircle enforces no normal flow. This is an analytical ideal-fluid solution, not a turbulent Navier–Stokes simulation.

Coordinates are normalized by the reference hill radius. Change U or a to explore the velocity field. The animation runs entirely in your browser.
